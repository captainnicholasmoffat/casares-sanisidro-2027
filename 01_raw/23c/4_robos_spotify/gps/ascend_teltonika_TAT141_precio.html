Reliable Connectivity and Precise Location

The TAT141 uses 4G LTE Cat M1 to ensure a stable and energy-efficient data connection in numerous countries. Periodic location reports provide regular updates on position, while the backup tracking function ensures continuous tracking even during temporary network outages. This way, you always maintain full visibility of your mobile assets—regardless of local network coverage.

Robust Design and Flexible Power Supply

Thanks to its extendable battery life, the tracker can be optimally adapted to different deployment durations without requiring frequent battery changes. The magnetic base allows tool-free, instant mounting on metallic surfaces—ideal for temporary or changing applications on containers, construction machinery, or vehicles. The compact housing reliably protects the electronics from mechanical influences.

Versatile Applications

The TAT141 is suitable for a wide range of tracking tasks: from cargo monitoring and vehicle supervision to locating heavy equipment and construction machinery. The combination of global LTE-M coverage, long battery life, and easy magnetic attachment makes it a universal solution for logistics, construction, and fleet management.
