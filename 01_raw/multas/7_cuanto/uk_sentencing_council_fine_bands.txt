For the purpose of the offence guidelines, a fine is usually based on one of three bands (A, B or C). The selection of the relevant fine band, and the position of the individual offence within that band, is determined by the seriousness of the offence. In some cases fine bands D – F may be used even where the community or custody threshold have been passed.

Starting point

Range

Fine Band A

 50% of relevant weekly income

 25 – 75% of relevant weekly income

Fine Band B

 100% of relevant weekly income

 75 – 125% of relevant weekly income

Fine Band C

 150% of relevant weekly income

125 – 175% of relevant weekly income

Fine Band D

 250% of relevant weekly income

200 – 300% of relevant weekly income

Fine Band E

400% of relevant weekly income

300 – 500% of relevant weekly income

Fine Band F

 600% of relevant weekly income

 500 – 700% of relevant weekly income

Maximum fines

A fine must not exceed the statutory limit. Where this is expressed in terms of a ‘level’, the maxima are:

Level 1

£200

Level 2

£500

Level 3

£1,000

Level 4

£2,500

Level 5

Unlimited (for offences committed after 13 March 2015)*

*For offences committed before 13 March 2015 the level 5 maximum is £5,000

See the 
Criminal Practice Directions 5.16
 for directions on dealing with cases involving very large fines in the magistrates’ court.
