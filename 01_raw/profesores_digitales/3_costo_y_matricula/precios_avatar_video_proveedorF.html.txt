 FAQs
 Got questions?
 How do conversation minutes work?
 Conversation minutes measure live CVI usage — the time an AI human is actively in a session with a user, from the API call connect to disconnect. Each conversation is rounded to the nearest 6-second increment, and incurs a 30-second minimum charge regardless of duration.
 ‍
 What's included in CVI? Are there additional services needed? 
 A conversation through CVI includes everything you need, from LLM to Audio to WebRTC. There are no additional services or charges required to run an end to end conversation.
It also includes all Tavus models: perception (Raven-1), conversation flow (Sparrow-2), and rendering (Phoenix-4.5).
 How to minimize CVI usage while testing?
 When a conversation creation request is sent, a conversation is initiated and billed, regardless of if a participant joins. As a safeguard in case a conversation isn’t properly closed, a conversation automatically closes after 5 minutes if a participant isn’t present, however these limits can be modified by setting the properties when creating a conversation.
 You can also enable test_mode = ‘TRUE’ to test the conversation creation process without incurring any costs.
 What happens if I go over my plan minutes?
 Most paid plans include the ability to have pay-as-you-go usage with no overage cap so you can use extra minutes. You’re billed only for what you use beyond your monthly allowance — never paused, never throttled. Additional usage is paid at the stated rate.
 What are concurrent sessions / streams?
 Concurrent sessions are the number of live CVI conversations that can run at the same time across your account. If you’re on a 3-session plan and a 4th user tries to start a conversation, they’ll need to wait for an active one to end. These limits are to ensure GPU availability for all users.
 There are many ways to gracefully handle concurrency limits such as waiting states or audio only modes.
 We often refer to this interchangeably between concurrency, streams, and sessions.
 Enterprise plans have custom limits — reach out if you need more capacity.
 What is async/video generation, and how is it different from CVI?
 CVI is Tavus’s primary product, and is a realtime conversational AI interface.
 Tavus also has video generation models available, which some customers use to additionally render MP4 videos as static assets (for ex: avatar video generation).
 ‍
 What's the difference between audio-only and audio+video pipeline modes?
 Tavus CVI can be deployed with the same pipeline to either audio only use cases or audio + video use cases. Customers often use both pipelines. Pricing is the same, although discounts for audio only will be introduced shortly.
 What's a custom PAL face?
 Custom faces are built from a single image or two minutes of training video. You can use a personal likeness, a character, or a brand-built persona. Faces live in your library and can be deployed across any number of conversations.
You have a certain number of custom faces available depending on your plan.

 What counts as a conversation minute?
 Conversation minutes measure live CVI usage — the time an AI human is actively in a session with a user, from connect to disconnect. They cover the full pipeline: perception (Raven-1), conversation flow (Sparrow-1), and rendering (Phoenix-4).
 What's a custom AI human replica?
 A replica is your own AI human — built from a single image or two minutes of training video. You can use a personal likeness or a brand-built persona. Replicas live in your library and can be deployed across any number of conversations.
 What happens if I go over my plan minutes?
 All paid plans include pay-as-you-go usage with no overage cap. You're billed only for what you use beyond your monthly allowance — never paused, never throttled.
 How does pay-as-you-go work?
 All Tavus plans include a certain amount of usage included, and additional usage is paid at the stated rate.
 Can I switch between plans? 
 Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel any time. Annual plans get a 20% discount and lock your rate in for the term.
