 15 U.S. Code § 6501 - Definitions

 In this chapter: 
 (1) Child 
 The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-94631196-710106869&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] child ” means an individual under the age of 13.
 (2) Operator The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-500553564-710106868&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] operator ”— 
 (A) means any [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-991716523-538476410&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] person who operates a website located on the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-635054945-710106864&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] Internet or an online service and who collects or maintains [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1252910924-710106862&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] personal information from or about the users of or visitors to such website or online service, or on whose behalf such information is collected or maintained, where such website or online service is operated for commercial purposes, including any [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-991716523-538476410&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] person offering products or services for sale through that website or online service, involving commerce— 
 (i) 
 among the several States or with 1 or more foreign nations;
 (ii) in any territory of the United States or in the District of Columbia, or between any such territory and— 
 (I) 
 another such territory; or
 (II) 
 any State or foreign nation; or
 (iii) 
 between the District of Columbia and any State, territory, or foreign nation; but
 (B) 
 does not include any nonprofit entity that would otherwise be exempt from coverage under [/uscode/text/15/45] section 45 of this title .
 (3) Commission 
 The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1283237621-710106867&term_occur=999&term_src=] Commission ” means the Federal Trade Commission .
 (4) Disclosure The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-445606381-710106866&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] disclosure ” means, with respect to [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1252910924-710106862&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] personal information — 
 (A) 
 the release of [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1252910924-710106862&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] personal information collected from a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-94631196-710106869&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] child in identifiable form by an [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-500553564-710106868&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] operator for any purpose, except where such information is provided to a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-991716523-538476410&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] person other than the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-500553564-710106868&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] operator who provides support for the internal operations of the website and does not disclose or use that information for any other purpose; and
 (B) making [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1252910924-710106862&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] personal information collected from a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-94631196-710106869&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] child by a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1202783540-2079753588&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] website or online service directed to children or with actual knowledge that such information was collected from a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-94631196-710106869&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] child, publicly available in identifiable form, by any means including by a public posting, through the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-635054945-710106864&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] Internet, or through— 
 (i) 
 a home page of a website;
 (ii) 
 a pen pal service;
 (iii) 
 an electronic mail service;
 (iv) 
 a message board; or
 (v) 
 a chat room.
 (5) Federal agency 
 The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1379945720-710106865&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] Federal agency ” means an agency, as that term is defined in [/uscode/text/5/551#1] section 551(1) of title 5 .
 (6) Internet 
 The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-635054945-710106864&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] Internet ” means collectively the myriad of computer and telecommunications facilities, including equipment and operating software, which comprise the interconnected world-wide network of networks that employ the Transmission Control Protocol/ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-635054945-710106864&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] Internet Protocol, or any predecessor or successor protocols to such protocol, to communicate information of all kinds by wire or radio.
 (7) Parent 
 The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-995424086-710106863&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] parent ” includes a legal guardian.
 (8) Personal information The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1252910924-710106862&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] personal information ” means individually identifiable information about an individual collected online, including— 
 (A) 
 a first and last name;
 (B) 
 a home or other physical address including street name and name of a city or town;
 (C) 
 an e-mail address;
 (D) 
 a telephone number;
 (E) 
 a Social Security number;
 (F) 
 any other identifier that the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1283237621-710106867&term_occur=999&term_src=] Commission determines permits the physical or online contacting of a specific individual; or
 (G) 
 information concerning the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-94631196-710106869&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] child or the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-995424086-710106863&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] parents of that [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-94631196-710106869&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] child that the website collects online from the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-94631196-710106869&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] child and combines with an identifier described in this paragraph.
 (9) Verifiable parental consent 
 The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-2002851468-710106861&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] verifiable parental consent ” means any reasonable effort (taking into consideration available technology), including a request for authorization for future collection, use, and [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-445606381-710106866&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] disclosure described in the notice, to ensure that a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-995424086-710106863&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] parent of a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-94631196-710106869&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] child receives notice of the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-500553564-710106868&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] operator’ s [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1252910924-710106862&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] personal information collection, use, and [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-445606381-710106866&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] disclosure practices, and authorizes the collection, use, and [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-445606381-710106866&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] disclosure, as applicable, of [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1252910924-710106862&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] personal information and the subsequent use of that information before that information is collected from that [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-94631196-710106869&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] child. 
 (10) Website or online service directed to children 
 (A) In general The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1202783540-2079753588&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] website or online service directed to children ” means— 
 (i) 
 a commercial website or online service that is targeted to children; or
 (ii) 
 that portion of a commercial website or online service that is targeted to children.
 (B) Limitation 
 A commercial website or online service, or a portion of a commercial website or online service, shall not be deemed directed to children solely for referring or linking to a commercial [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1202783540-2079753588&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] website or online service directed to children by using information location tools, including a directory, index, reference, pointer, or hypertext link.
 (11) Person 
 The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-991716523-538476410&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] person ” means any individual, partnership, corporation, trust, estate, cooperative, association, or other entity.
 (12) Online contact information 
 The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-1710018593-538476409&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] online contact information ” means an e-mail address or another substantially similar identifier that permits direct contact with a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=15-USC-991716523-538476410&term_occur=999&term_src=title:15:chapter:91:section:6501] person online.

 “Sections [/uscode/text/15/1303#a] 1303(a) , [/uscode/text/15/1305] 1305 , and [/uscode/text/15/1306] 1306 of this title [enacting sections [/uscode/text/15/6502#a] 6502(a) , [/uscode/text/15/6504] 6504 , and [/uscode/text/15/6505] 6505 of this title] take effect on the later of—
