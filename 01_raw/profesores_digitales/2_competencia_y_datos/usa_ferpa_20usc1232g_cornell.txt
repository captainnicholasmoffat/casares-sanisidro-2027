 (a) Conditions for availability of funds to educational agencies or institutions; inspection and review of education records; specific information to be made available; procedure for access to education records; reasonableness of time for such access; hearings; written explanations by parents; definitions 
 (1) 
 (A) 
 No funds shall be made available under any [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1607909629-1113929722&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] applicable program to any [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution which has a policy of denying, or which effectively prevents, the parents of [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] students who are or have been in attendance at a school of such agency or at such institution, as the case may be, the right to inspect and review the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-636090826-1128590670&term_occur=999&term_src=] education records of their children. If any material or document in the education record of a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] student includes information on more than one [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] student, the parents of one of such [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] students shall have the right to inspect and review only such part of such material or document as relates to such [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] student or to be informed of the specific information contained in such part of such material. Each [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution shall establish appropriate procedures for the granting of a request by parents for access to the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-636090826-1128590670&term_occur=999&term_src=] education records of their children within a reasonable period of time, but in no case more than forty-five days after the request has been made.
 (B) 
 No funds under any [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1607909629-1113929722&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] applicable program shall be made available to any State educational agency (whether or not that agency is an [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution under this section) that has a policy of denying, or effectively prevents, the parents of [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] students the right to inspect and review the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-636090826-1128590670&term_occur=999&term_src=] education records maintained by the State educational agency on their children who are or have been in attendance at any school of an [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution that is subject to the provisions of this section.
 (C) The first sentence of subparagraph (A) shall not operate to make available to [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] students in institutions of postsecondary education the following materials: 
 (i) 
 financial records of the parents of the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student or any information contained therein;
 (ii) 
 confidential letters and statements of recommendation, which were placed in the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-636090826-1128590670&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] education records prior to January 1, 1975 , if such letters or statements are not used for purposes other than those for which they were specifically intended;
 (iii) if the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student has signed a waiver of the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student ’s right of access under this subsection in accordance with subparagraph (D), confidential recommendations— 
 (I) 
 respecting admission to any [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution ,
 (II) 
 respecting an application for employment, and
 (III) 
 respecting the receipt of an honor or honorary recognition.
 (D) 
 A [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student or a person applying for admission may waive his right of access to confidential statements described in clause (iii) of subparagraph (C), except that such waiver shall apply to recommendations only if (i) the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student is, upon request, notified of the names of all persons making confidential recommendations and (ii) such recommendations are used solely for the purpose for which they were specifically intended. Such waivers may not be required as a condition for admission to, receipt of financial aid from, or receipt of any other services or benefits from such agency or institution.
 (2) 
 No funds shall be made available under any [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1607909629-1113929722&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] applicable program to any [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution unless the parents of [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] students who are or have been in attendance at a school of such agency or at such institution are provided an opportunity for a hearing by such agency or institution, in accordance with regulations of the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1264422296-1113929725&term_occur=999&term_src=] Secretary, to challenge the content of such [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] student’ s [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-636090826-1128590670&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] education records, in order to insure that the records are not inaccurate, misleading, or otherwise in violation of the privacy rights of [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] students, and to provide an opportunity for the correction or deletion of any such inaccurate, misleading or otherwise inappropriate data contained therein and to insert into such records a written explanation of the parents respecting the content of such records.
 (3) 
 For the purposes of this section the term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution ” means any public or private agency or institution which is the recipient of funds under any [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1607909629-1113929722&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] applicable program. 
 (4) 
 (A) For the purposes of this section, the term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-636090826-1128590670&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] education records ” means, except as may be provided otherwise in subparagraph (B), those records, files, documents, and other materials which— 
 (i) 
 contain information directly related to a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student ; and
 (ii) 
 are maintained by an [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution or by a person acting for such agency or institution.
 (B) The term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-636090826-1128590670&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] education records ” does not include— 
 (i) 
 records of instructional, supervisory, and administrative personnel and educational personnel ancillary thereto which are in the sole possession of the maker thereof and which are not accessible or revealed to any other person except a substitute;
 (ii) 
 records maintained by a law enforcement unit of the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution that were created by that law enforcement unit for the purpose of law enforcement;
 (iii) 
 in the case of persons who are employed by an [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution but who are not in attendance at such agency or institution, records made and maintained in the normal course of business which relate exclusively to such person in that person’s capacity as an employee and are not available for use for any other purpose; or
 (iv) 
 records on a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student who is eighteen years of age or older, or is attending an institution of postsecondary education , which are made or maintained by a physician, psychiatrist, psychologist, or other recognized professional or paraprofessional acting in his professional or paraprofessional capacity, or assisting in that capacity, and which are made, maintained, or used only in connection with the provision of treatment to the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] student, and are not available to anyone other than persons providing such treatment, except that such records can be personally reviewed by a physician or other appropriate professional of the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] student’ s choice.
 (5) 
 (A) 
 For the purposes of this section the term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-716702713-1128590671&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] directory information ” relating to a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] student includes the following: the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] student’ s name, address, telephone listing, date and place of birth, major field of study, participation in officially recognized activities and sports, weight and height of members of athletic teams, dates of attendance, degrees and awards received, and the most recent previous [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution attended by the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student. 
 (B) 
 Any [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution making public [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-716702713-1128590671&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] directory information shall give public notice of the categories of information which it has designated as such information with respect to each [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] student attending the institution or agency and shall allow a reasonable period of time after such notice has been given for a parent to inform the institution or agency that any or all of the information designated should not be released without the parent’s prior consent.
 (6) 
 For the purposes of this section, the term “ [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student ” includes any person with respect to whom an [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution maintains [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-636090826-1128590670&term_occur=999&term_src=] education records or personally identifiable information, but does not include a person who has not been in attendance at such agency or institution.
 (b) Release of education records; parental consent requirement; exceptions; compliance with judicial orders and subpoenas; audit and evaluation of federally-supported education programs; recordkeeping 
 (1) No funds shall be made available under any [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1607909629-1113929722&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] applicable program to any [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution which has a policy or practice of permitting the release of [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-636090826-1128590670&term_occur=999&term_src=] education records (or personally identifiable information contained therein other than [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-716702713-1128590671&term_occur=999&term_src=] directory information, as defined in paragraph (5) of subsection (a)) of [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] students without the written consent of their parents to any individual, agency, or organization, other than to the following— 
 (A) 
 other school officials, including teachers within the educational institution or local educational agency, who have been determined by such agency or institution to have legitimate educational interests, including the educational interests of the child for whom consent would otherwise be required;
 (B) 
 officials of other schools or school systems in which the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student seeks or intends to enroll, upon condition that the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student ’s parents be notified of the transfer, receive a copy of the record if desired, and have an opportunity for a hearing to challenge the content of the record;
 (C) 
 (i) 
 authorized representatives of (I) the Comptroller General of the United States, (II) the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1264422296-1113929725&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] Secretary , or (III) State educational authorities, under the conditions set forth in paragraph (3), or (ii) authorized representatives of the Attorney General for law enforcement purposes under the same conditions as apply to the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1264422296-1113929725&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] Secretary under paragraph (3);
 (D) 
 in connection with a [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student ’s application for, or receipt of, financial aid;
 (E) State and local officials or authorities to whom such information is specifically allowed to be reported or disclosed pursuant to State statute adopted— 
 (i) 
 before November 19, 1974 , if the allowed reporting or disclosure concerns the juvenile justice system and such system’s ability to effectively serve the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=] student whose records are released, or
 (ii) after November 19, 1974 , if— 
 (I) 
 the allowed reporting or disclosure concerns the juvenile justice system and such system’s ability to effectively serve, prior to adjudication, the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student whose records are released; and
 (II) 
 the officials and authorities to whom such information is disclosed certify in writing to the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-360690153-1128590669&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] educational agency or institution that the information will not be disclosed to any other party except as provided under State law without the prior written consent of the parent of the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student. [#fn002121] [1] 
 (F) 
 organizations conducting studies for, or on behalf of, educational agencies or institutions for the purpose of developing, validating, or administering predictive tests, administering [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student aid programs, and improving instruction, if such studies are conducted in such a manner as will not permit the personal identification of [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] students and their parents by persons other than representatives of such organizations and such information will be destroyed when no longer needed for the purpose for which it is conducted;
 (G) 
 accrediting organizations in order to carry out their accrediting functions;
 (H) 
 parents of a dependent [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student of such parents, as defined in [/uscode/text/26/152] section 152 of title 26 ;
 (I) 
 subject to regulations of the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1264422296-1113929725&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] Secretary , in connection with an emergency, appropriate persons if the knowledge of such information is necessary to protect the health or safety of the [/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=20-USC-1879145925-1128590672&term_occur=999&term_src=title:20:chapter:31:subchapter:III:part:4:section:1232g] student or other persons;
 (J) 
 (i)
