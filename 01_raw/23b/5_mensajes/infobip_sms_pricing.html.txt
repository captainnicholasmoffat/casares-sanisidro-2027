 Frequently asked questions about SMS pricing
 How does message volume and frequency affect the overall cost of SMS messaging?
 The number of messages you send (volume) and how often you send them (frequency) can affect your costs. Sending a lot of messages might get you discounts, but sending them too often can increase costs if not managed well.
 The SMS provider you choose also impacts costs. Some offer additional features like automation and personalization, which can make your campaigns better but might cost more.
 Tip : Watch out for extra fees that can add to your costs. Some companies charge for things like setup or special support. Knowing about these fees helps you understand the true cost of your SMS messaging & marketing. By keeping these factors in mind, you can plan your strategy to get the best results without overspending.
 Why do SMS costs vary by location?
 SMS costs can change depending on where you’re sending messages. Different countries have different rates, and these can affect your overall budget. Local mobile carrier fees also play a big role in these costs, as some carriers charge more for sending messages in certain areas. SMS providers negotiate rates with carriers, and these agreements can influence the cost. Providers with strong relationships and agreements with carriers, like Infobip, may offer more competitive pricing.
 To manage these costs, it’s important to have a strategy. One way is to check the specific SMS rates for the countries you want to target, like you can do above in our calculator . This helps you understand what you’ll be paying and allows you to plan your budget better.
 What are the price differences between SMS and MMS?
 SMS is a text-only service limited to 160 characters , while MMS allows for multimedia content and longer messages. MMS typically costs more due to the added features and content options.
 Does the number of contacts (contact list) affect SMS pricing?
 The size of your contact list can affect your SMS pricing plan. The more contacts you want to send messages to (meaning, the more messages you send), the greater the cost. However, at Infobip we offer discounts for enterprises who want to send large volumes of messages. Feel free to get in touch with our team to learn more.
 What are the drawbacks of choosing low-cost SMS providers?
 Choosing the lowest-priced SMS provider may seem attractive, but it often comes with hidden drawbacks. Such providers might use inferior routes that can impact the quality and reliability of message delivery, resulting in delayed or undelivered messages. With Infobip, you can trust in a high-quality, reliable service that ensures your messages reach their destination efficiently.
