Discover the pioneer in floating litter capture technology

The Bandalong Litter Trap™ has been a groundbreaking solution in water pollution control since the 1990s, setting the standard for nearly three decades. Engineered with resilience and sustainability in mind, it has become the trusted choice for organizations dedicated to the long-term health of aquatic water systems.

The Bandalong Litter Trap™ is an in-stream device, expertly designed to capture floating litter and debris in waterways before they can reach the ocean. It is highly effective in rivers, streams, creeks, lakes, drainage culverts and other waterways with a current.  Importantly, Bandalong Litter Traps do not impact channel hydraulics or flood levels and are specially crafted to ensure they do not impede the movement of aquatic wildlife.

Why Bandalong Litter Trap™?

The Bandalong Litter Trap™ operates 24/7 without mechanical aid, utilizing natural water currents to capture litter efficiently.

Continuous Environmental Protection

This robust tool guarantees uninterrupted operation, making it a reliable solution for long-term water pollution control. Each model is expertly crafted to meet the unique demands of various waterway environments. The Bandalong's specially designed floating structure rises and falls with the water’s surface, allowing it to adapt seamlessly to changes in water level, including complete seasonal drought. This ensures your commitment to environmental stewardship is consistently upheld.

Innovative Capture Technology

Our traps leverage natural water currents to efficiently guide debris into the collection system, operating continuously 24/7, 365 days a year without the need for mechanical intervention. The dynamic floating design automatically adjusts to changing water levels, ensuring effective performance in a wide range of environmental conditions while preserving aquatic life and water flow integrity.

Efficient Debris Direction

Collection booms extend outward to direct debris into the trap efficiently. In tidal areas, optional tidal gates keep litter contained, using a system of counterweights and paddles to close the entry gate when water flow stops or changes direction due to tide or wind. These traps are designed for ease of access, supporting multiple debris removal methods, including mechanical, manual, and vacuum extraction, ensuring versatility in maintenance.

Our Models

The Bandalong Boss

Model 320

Compact and efficient, the Bandalong Boss #320 is designed for adaptability across diverse settings, making it ideal for managing moderate debris levels. It combines efficiency with robust design, featuring 12" diameter HDPE flotation and collection booms with a flexible draft skirt. The aluminum structural frame, rear gate, and optional lift-out basket ensures durability and ease of maintenance.  The Boss is well suited for flashy urban channels with velocities up to 10 feet per second and widths up to 200 feet at the top of the bank.

Dimensions: 7’ wide x 18’ long.

The Bandalong Bulldog

Model 920

The Bandalong Bulldog #920 offers enhanced capacity for medium to heavy volumes of litter, featuring triple pontoons and reinforced cross-brace frames that boost both durability and stability in diverse conditions. Designed for flashy urban channels with velocities up to 10 feet per second and widths up to 200 feet at the top of the bank, it includes 12” diameter HDPE flotation and collection booms with a flexible draft skirt. The aluminum structural frame, rear gate, and optional lift-out baskets (2) provide robust support, while optional aluminum handrails and walkways offer additional safety and accessibility

Dimensions: 16’ wide x 31’ long

The Bandalong Beast

Model 922

Engineered for maximum containment in the most demanding environments, the Bandalong Beast #922 is capable of handling up to 8,000 pounds of debris. Its strength and buoyancy have been verified by Finite Element Analysis (FEA), ensuring it remains operational even under heavy debris loads. Designed for flashy urban channels with velocities up to 10 feet per second and widths up to 200 feet at the top of the bank, it features 12” diameter HDPE flotation and collection booms with a flexible draft skirt. The aluminum structural frame, rear gate, and optional lift-out baskets (2) provide robust support, with optional aluminum handrails and walkways available for added safety and accessibility.

Dimensions: 16’ wide x 50’ long.

Bandalong Bulldog – Model #920

This video highlights the Bandalong Bulldog Model #920, installed in a drainage channel in Huntington Beach, CA, in 2016. Measuring 16 feet wide by 30 feet long, the Bulldog, like other full-size Bandalong Litter Traps, features 12-inch HDPE booms and 6061-T6 aluminum structural members. The aluminum is powder-coated to deter theft, ensuring durability and security.

For this installation, pilings were used for anchoring, a method selected based on site-specific engineering requirements. The Bandalong Bulldog empties into the Bolsa Chica Ecological Reserve and the Pacific Ocean, incorporating a tidal gate to prevent trapped litter from escaping during tidal changes. Operating in environments ranging from fresh to brackish and saltwater, the Bulldog provides effective litter capture across diverse conditions.

Bandalong Beast – Model #922

This video showcases the unloading of the Bandalong Beast, our robust Model #922, which measures 16 feet wide by 50 feet long. Engineered for durability, the Bandalong Beast underwent Finite Element Analysis (FEA) by a consulting engineer, confirming its capacity to support up to 8,000 pounds while remaining afloat. Although encountering such a massive load of floatables may be rare, this trap is built to handle woody debris, grass, and other materials from miles of open channels.Installed in a 200-foot-wide channel, the site required 400 linear feet of Bandalong Collection Boom, featuring a 6-inch flexible draft skirt to capture partially submerged litter and debris. The Bandalong Beast, though snug in the channel’s low flow, effectively manages peak rain events that fill the channel to the top of the bank.Over the past seven years, this Bandalong Beast has captured everything from couches and tons of wet grass to various other floatables. Discarded household items and litter regularly travel downstream during significant rain events, only to be intercepted by this powerful system.Cleanouts are efficiently managed with a crane, utilizing the two lift-out baskets available as an option for the Bandalong Beast.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
for Bandalong Litter Traps™

What makes the Bandalong Litter Trap™ unique?

The Bandalong Litter Traps™ stand out for their innovative floating design that utilizes natural water currents to efficiently capture debris without mechanical aid. This passive operation allows for continuous debris management, making it a sustainable and effective solution for reducing water pollution.

How do the Bandalong Litter Trap™ capture and manage debris?

All Bandalong Litter Traps™ harness natural water currents to guide debris into the collection system, operating continuously. The dynamic floating design adapts to water level changes, ensuring effective operation across various environmental conditions without disturbing      aquatic life or water flow.

What materials are used in the construction of the Bandalong Litter Traps™?

Bandalong Litter Traps™ are constructed from high-density polyethylene (HDPE) and marine-grade aluminum, ensuring durability and longevity. These materials are chosen for their resistance to corrosion and their ability to withstand harsh environmental conditions.

How durable are the Bandalong Litter Traps™?

Designed for resilience, the Bandalong Litter Traps™ are built to last, crafted from corrosion-resistant materials that endure varying weather and water conditions. Their robust construction guarantees long-term performance and minimal maintenance.

What are the environmental impacts of using Bandalong Litter Trap™?

Bandalong Litter Traps™ significantly contribute to environmental conservation by capturing and removing floating debris before it can harm aquatic ecosystems. Their operation helps preserve local wildlife and enhances the natural beauty of waterways, promoting biodiversity and ecological health.

How do Bandalong Litter Traps™ fit into various environments?

Bandalong Litter Traps™ are adaptable to a wide range of waterway settings, from small urban streams to large rivers. Their      floating design allows for easy installation in diverse environments without the need for significant infrastructural changes.

What is the installation process for Bandalong Litter Traps™?

Installation of Bandalong Litter Traps™ is straightforward and can be tailored to specific site conditions. All Bandalong Litter Traps™ are installed by our team of professionals at Storm Water Systems, this ensures that the trap is installed correctly and meets all of our warranty requirements.

How often do Bandalong Litter Traps™ require maintenance?

Maintenance needs are generally low; however, regular checks are recommended to ensure the traps are free from obstructions and are operating efficiently. The frequency of maintenance will depend on the debris levels and the specific environment where the trap is installed.

Can Bandalong Litter Traps™ be customized to fit specific site conditions?

Yes, Bandalong Litter Traps™ offer flexibility in terms of customization. We provide options to modify dimensions and components to best fit the requirements of different waterways and debris challenges.

What warranty and support options are available for Bandalong Litter Traps™?

All Bandalong Litter Traps™ are backed by a dual-tier warranty: a 1-year warranty on metal components and installation, and a 10-year warranty on the pontoons and booms. This ensures long-term reliability and performance in various waterway conditions. Additionally, our dedicated support team is available to provide ongoing assistance, helping you to maximize the effectiveness of your litter trap and address any operational concerns that may arise.
