Joining Sakana AI.


We are looking for exceptional talent to join our small but ambitious lab in Tokyo. Browse the open positions below and apply for the role that fits you best.

エンタープライズ・ソリューションのポジションにご興味がある方は、Applied Teamの採用情報をご覧ください。

How to Apply

  1. 1
    Find a role

    Review the open positions and identify the one that fits your background.

  2. 2
    Email careers@sakana.ai

    Send a brief introduction and indicate which role you are applying for.

  3. 3
    Submit the form

    Complete the Google Form with your CV and cover letter (English).

Scam warning: Sakana AI recruiters contact candidates only from @sakana.ai email addresses. Be cautious of messages from Gmail or other free providers. See our notices on Twitter and LinkedIn.
