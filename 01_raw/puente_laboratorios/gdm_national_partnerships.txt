Our National Partnerships for AI
Working with governments worldwide to benefit people through frontier AI
 Your browser does not support the video tag.
We believe AI will be humanity's most transformative technology. We’re partnering with governments globally to turn its potential into real progress for people everywhere. Our global initiative helps achieve this – supporting governments to better serve their people through frontier AI.
We’re broadening access to our research and technologies to help meet national priorities— from advancing scientific discovery, to driving public sector efficiency, and empowering those working at the forefront of public services.
It’s all part of our mission to build AI responsibly to benefit humanity.
Our approach
We believe AI has the biggest impact on citizens’ lives when it’s developed with the institutions who serve those individuals. Our partnerships are tailored to meet each country’s unique context and goals, and are guided by five principles:
Promoting access and local opportunity:
 Through broadening access to our frontier AI technologies, research, and expertise, we support national ambitions that are aligned to our 
company mission
 and 
principles
Reimagining public services:
 We help reimagine public services so they enhance people’s daily lives and create resilient societies ready to navigate the future
Advancing scientific breakthroughs:
 We help experts tackle some of their country’s greatest challenges, and support future scientific breakthroughs, by broadening access to our cutting-edge technologies and leading research
Enhancing education:
 We work to expand learning practices and improve educational outcomes by developing AI that's deeply informed by pedagogical research, grounded in learning science and supported by experts
Advancing security and resilience:
 We advance security, working with governments to manage risks and build public trust through cutting-edge capability research and AI-enhanced approaches to security and resilience

Working together for the future
These partnerships are rooted in our belief that unlocking AI’s potential requires more than building the technology. It requires deep, strategic collaboration between the developers of frontier AI and the countries that will deploy it.
Together, we can accelerate progress responsibly, and prepare societies for an AI-enabled future.
We’re always looking for new partners who want to build a more prosperous, secure future for us all.
