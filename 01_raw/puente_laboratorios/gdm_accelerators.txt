About our programs
Our equity-free programs support the work of AI-focused startups, developers, research labs, and non-profit organizations. We give them access to our experts and most advanced AI stacks, so that they can cross the gap between lab discoveries and market ventures.
Specialized frontier models
We give participants early or exclusive access to frontier models and specialized research tools to support their work. Selected participants could also be eligible to receive product credits.
Expertise and technical partnerships
We match participants with Google engineers, experts, and researchers to help solve key technical challenges. Our teams offer strategic advice on tech stacks, product design, and future growth.
Global exposure
Participants get to share their work at an accelerator Demo Day. They may also have the opportunity to have their work featured at flagship Google events like I/O and other industry moments.

Google Accelerators
Since 2016, 
Google Accelerators
 have supported the top AI-first startups, developers and non-profit organizations around the world. Google Accelerators operate across 6 continents, supporting technology leaders in 88 countries, with a portfolio that now includes over 2,000 startups and organizations.
Google Accelerators focus on working with brilliant people building products and technologies that solve meaningful problems. Portfolio companies get premier access to the best of Google - expert mentorship, products, platforms, and support for building AI responsibly - to grow and advance their businesses.
