What makes us different?

Unicorn Factory Lisboa acts along the entire value chain to have a strong economic impact on the City of Lisbon.

Future Pipeline

Education programs and partnerships with universities.

Startup

Acceleration and incubation programs.

Scaleup

Scaleup acceleration programs and international scaleup attraction.

Specialization

Synergies and networking in vertical hubs with critical mass.

Unicorns

We nurture an active and collaborative peer community as well as large mentor and investor network.

International Opportunities

Access to international summits and other top global opportunities.

We are committed to creating a strong, dynamic and sustainable ecosystem where businesses of all stages can thrive, from young entrepreneurs to established unicorns.
