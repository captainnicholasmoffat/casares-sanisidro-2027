Role Title
: University Interns

Candidate Eligibility
: Candidate must be enrolled in a full time bachelor's, masters, MBA, or PhD program in area relevant for the role during the academic term immediately before their internship. 

Program Rules
: Candidate must be returning to school for 1 full academic term post internship.

Candidate can not complete 2 back to back internships with Microsoft.

Candidate can not be taking classes while interning with Microsoft.

Candidate must be at least 16 years of age.
