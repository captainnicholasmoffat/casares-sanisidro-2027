Frequently asked questions

What is ByteDance's overall research direction or focus?

ByteDance has two primary research focuses: foundational research in machine learning and AI exploring broad applications for both current and future consumer needs, and applied research focused on delivering practical AI solutions for immediate product challenges. We remain committed to our long-term tech vision, prioritizing meaningful technological progress and investing in technologies that truly matter.

If I'm not a part of a foundational research team, will I have the opportunity to publish research papers?

Researchers across foundational and applied research teams will have the opportunity to publish papers, provided they follow the required internal review and approval process.

Are PhD positions only available in the research function?

We value the expertise PhD talent brings and offer roles for both researchers and non-researchers. Non-research roles let PhDs apply complex machine learning models to real business challenges, driving meaningful impact. This hands-on industry experience complements academic research, creating a well-rounded career journey.

Are there opportunities for continuous learning and development after I join the company?

Yes, the company supports continuous learning, and many teams regularly attend research conferences to stay current with industry advancements. These conferences also provide opportunities for our teams to share their work through publications.
