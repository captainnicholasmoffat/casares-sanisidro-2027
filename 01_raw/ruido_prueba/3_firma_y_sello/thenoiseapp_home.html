Digital innovation to transform noise investigations

The Noise App processes millions of noise reports every year. We have the expertise, backed up with long-term investment in people and quality software, to continue evolving and deliver the best solution for professionals and citizens.

The Noise App provides assurance with standardised and validated data capture and high-quality audio, video and imagery. Citizens and professionals can report, triage and manage noise issues with confidence. Investigators and consultants can use the app to corroborate public reports or to create and manage noise projects.

The Noise App has proven effective in collecting and managing data in a secure environment. The judiciary accepts it as admissible evidence in legal hearings. The Noise App has been successfully used to present evidence in court for statutory enforcement and in civil cases, including safeguarding, civil injunctions and mediation.

The Noise App processes more than a million noise nuisance reports every year. It is the preferred solution for citizens reporting noise and anti-social behaviour. Independent research shows that capturing and sharing evidence of noise as it happens increases transparency and public confidence in the investigative process. Citizens can download the app for free if their investigating organisation subscribes to it; check with your provider to find out.
