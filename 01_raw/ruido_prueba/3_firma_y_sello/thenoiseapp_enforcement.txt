 UK Enforcement
 The Noise App was designed to ensure integrity of data capture, compliance with regulatory frameworks and reliability of that data for use as evidence. Since 2015, evidence collected using The Noise App has been successfully presented in legal actions, including hundreds of court cases. Examples listed below demonstrate successful enforcement action using The Noise App in England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
 The Noise Workspace
 The Noise App provides a single workspace for collaborative noise case management.
Our platform provides complete visibility of noise-related issues and has proven to be an essential tool for cities, communities and professionals in noise control and community safety.
