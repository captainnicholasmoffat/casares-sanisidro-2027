Step 1 
Download The Noise App 2.0 from 
your Google Play or Apple App store 
and follow the guidance provided 
 
Step 2 
Create your account and select the 
reporting organisation you wish to submit 
your noise complaint to 
 
Step 3 
Start recording the noise you are 
experiencing and capture your evidence in 
real time 
 
Step 4 
Await notifications regarding updates on 
your case and continue to report as 
necessary
