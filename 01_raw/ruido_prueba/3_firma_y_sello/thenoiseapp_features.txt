 Transforming data capture and analysis
 Transforming data capture and analysis
 Better data
 The Noise App captures multiple simultaneous real-time data points from the user and the device to deliver an accurate picture of each case. Data points include audio, photo, video, geolocation, postcode + UPRN, time, date and many more to support accurate investigation and analysis. 
Audio data is taken directly from the smartphone microphone, bypassing inbuilt noise suppression and cancellation software. This enables a wide range of applications from acoustic experts using frequency analysis to solve complex tonal noise issues, through to housing providers assessing the subjective impact of noise nuisance or anti-social behaviour.

 Verified data
 To ensure that The Noise App can be used effectively right through to enforcement action, we collect the data with a complete, verified audit trail. When a report is submitted, the exact time, date and GPS location-specific data sets across the reporting device, and our servers are cross-referenced.
This means that crucial data cannot be altered and can be relied on in court. Every evidence update on the app or the platform is labelled, identifying who and when, to give a complete case timeline.

 NEW FEATURE
 Audio Classifier
 Submitted recordings are automatically analysed to identify likely sound events. These events appear in the web app audio player, so officers can move directly to the most relevant sections instead of repeatedly searching through a full recording.
 Less time manually navigating audio. Faster initial triage. More time to process investigations.
 Find the signal faster
 Detected sound events are surfaced in the recording player.
 How audio classification works
 There are no new steps for officers to complete. Classification takes place automatically when a reporter submits a recording through The Noise App.
 01
 Analyses the submitted audio
 02
 Detects likely sound events
 03
 Matches events to the reported noise
 04
 Displays relevant moments in the audio player
 05
 Allows officers to move directly between detected sections
 The classifier uses noise source categories already supported by The Noise App, including barking dogs, construction noise and car alarms. Officers remain in control of reviewing the recording, considering its relevance and deciding how the report should progress.
