Overview

Peru Beach is a kitesurfing spot located on the Rio de la Plata, in the San Isidro area, north of Buenos Aires. The shallow brown waters of the estuary offer accessible conditions. Suitable for intermediate riders, the spot benefits from southeast winds (Sudestada) and northeast winds with tidal influence on the water.

Peru Beach: when to go?

Rideable days and hours computed for a minimum wind of 7 knots · log in to set your own (foil, twin-tip…)

The season runs from October to February, peaking in January: 20 rideable days a month and 134 hours of usable wind, at 26 °C. The low point is July, at 10 days.
