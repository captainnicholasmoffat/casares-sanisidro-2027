Summer Breeze Sets Record Ridership In Laguna Beach

Did you ride the Summer Breeze this summer?

Ashley Ludwig, Patch Staff
Posted Sat, Aug 12, 2017 at 1:00 am PT|Updated Sat, Aug 12, 2017 at 4:00 am PT

LAGUNA BEACH, CA — Summer Breeze gives riders the chance to sit back and relax rather than be unnerved by beach traffic in Laguna. This year, ridership was up, well over 15% the city reported.

"The City's Summer Breeze program has already surpassed last year’s total ridership by 18% with four more weeks of service remaining," Laguna Beach City Manager John Pietig said in recent release. "The Summer Breeze provides free parking and a free bus ride into Laguna Beach from a parking lot located at the 405 freeway and SR-133."

There's still time to catch a ride into Laguna, with the OC Bus Service running until September 3, Saturday & Sunday from 11 a.m. - midnight
