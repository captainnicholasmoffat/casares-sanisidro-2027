Total Parameters
The total number of trainable weights and biases in the model, expressed in billions. These parameters are learned during training and determine the model's ability to process and generate responses.
Active Parameters at Inference Time
The number of parameters actually executed during each inference forward pass, expressed in billions. For Mixture of Experts (MoE) models, a routing mechanism selects a subset of experts per token, resulting in fewer active than total parameters. Dense models use all parameters, so active equals total.
Frequently Asked Questions
Which is the most intelligent AI model?
Claude Opus 5.5 (Max, Default Fallback) currently leads the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with a score of 58, out of 197 models evaluated.
What are the top AI models?
The top AI models by Intelligence Index are: 1. Claude Opus 5.5 (Max, Default Fallback) (58), 2. Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Max, Default Fallback) (56), 3. Claude Opus 5.5 (Xhigh, Default Fallback) (56), 4. Claude Opus 5.5 (High, Default Fallback) (54), and 5. Claude Fable 5.1 (Max, Default Fallback) (53).
Which is the fastest AI model?
Celeris-1 is the fastest at 1,767.4 tokens per second, followed by Mercury 2 (739.3 t/s) and Mercury 2.5 (661.4 t/s).
Which is the cheapest AI model?
Llama 3.1 Instruct 8B is the most affordable at $0.02 per 1M tokens (blended), followed by Granite 4.2 3B ($0.02) and Nova Micro ($0.03).
Which AI model has the lowest latency?
Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite (Non-reasoning) has the lowest time to first token at 0.31s, followed by Nemotron 3 Nano Omni 30B A3B Reasoning (0.36s) and North Mini Code (0.42s).
Which is the best open weights AI model?
MiMo-V2.6-Pro is the highest-ranked open weights model with an Intelligence Index score of 46. There are 81 open weights models out of 197 total evaluated.
What are the top open weights AI models?
The top open weights AI models by Intelligence Index are: 1. MiMo-V2.6-Pro (46), 2. GLM-5.3 (Max) (45), and 3. Kimi K3 (Max) (44).
Which is the best reasoning model?
Claude Opus 5.5 (Max, Default Fallback) leads among 175 reasoning models with an Intelligence Index score of 58. Reasoning models use extended thinking to work through complex problems before providing answers.
How are AI models compared on Artificial Analysis?
Models are compared across multiple dimensions including intelligence (quality), pricing, output speed (tokens per second), latency (time to first token), end-to-end response time, and context window size. Performance metrics are measured directly using standardized prompts across 690 models.
How do I compare a specific model against others?
Click on any model name or row in the charts to view its dedicated page with detailed metrics and direct comparisons against similar models. You can also use the model selector to customize which models appear in each chart.
