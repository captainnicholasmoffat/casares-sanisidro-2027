CAPTCHA-Free Verification

Cloudflare Turnstile confirms web visitors are real and blocks unwanted bots without slowing down web experiences for real users. This is achieved with a simple snippet of free code that eliminates the need for frustrating CAPTCHAs.

Enhanced User Privacy

Unlike other CAPTCHA options, Turnstile never harvests data for ad retargeting. This preserves the privacy of your users without sacrificing the effectiveness of bot blocking.

Improved User Experience

Users no longer have to waste time solving visual puzzles. Turnstile works transparently to confirm they are real people, providing a smoother experience.

Streamline Visitor Verification
Turnstile provides a seamless way to verify web visitors and block bots without impacting user experience. It's easy to integrate and enhances privacy.
