Comparison Summary
DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) is amongst the leading models in intelligence and reasonably priced when comparing to other open weight models of similar size. It's also notably fast, however very verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.
DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) scores 39 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 19). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 250M tokens, which is very verbose in comparison to the median of 140M.
Pricing for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) is $0.30 per 1M input tokens (moderately priced, median: $0.30) and $1.20 per 1M output tokens (somewhat expensive, median: $1.13). On average, it costs $0.27 per task to evaluate DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) on the Intelligence Index.
At 214 tokens per second, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) is notably fast (71).

Total Parameters
The total number of trainable weights and biases in the model, expressed in billions. These parameters are learned during training and determine the model's ability to process and generate responses.
Active Parameters at Inference Time
The number of parameters actually executed during each inference forward pass, expressed in billions. For Mixture of Experts (MoE) models, a routing mechanism selects a subset of experts per token, resulting in fewer active than total parameters. Dense models use all parameters, so active equals total.

How intelligent is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max)?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) generates output at 214.1 tokens per second (based on DeepSeek's API), which is well above average compared to other open weight models of similar size (median: 71.4 t/s).
What is the latency of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max)?
DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) has a time to first token (TTFT) of 1.05s (based on DeepSeek's API), which is very competitive compared to other open weight models of similar size (median: 2.14s).

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) costs $0.30 per 1M input tokens (better than average, median: $0.43) and $1.20 per 1M output tokens (better than average, median: $1.68), based on DeepSeek's API.

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) costs $0.30 per 1M input tokens and $1.20 per 1M output tokens (based on DeepSeek's API). For a blended rate (7:2:1 cache hit/input/output ratio), this is $0.18 per 1M tokens. Pricing may vary by provider.

Is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) multimodal?

What is the context window of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max)?
DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) has a context window of 1.0M tokens. This determines how much text and conversation history the model can process in a single request.
