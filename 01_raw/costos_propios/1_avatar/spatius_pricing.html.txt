 How billing works
 Credits map to minutes
 Every 10 credits represents roughly one avatar minute. Plan capacity is shown as an equivalent monthly allowance.
 Annual plans lower the rate
 Annual billing is paid once per year and saves 20% against the monthly plan price.
 Paid plans can flex
 Continue past included usage at the plan overage rate, and purchase extra avatar creations separately.
 Custom avatar slots are unlimited on paid plans during Beta. Pricing and limits may change as the product evolves.
