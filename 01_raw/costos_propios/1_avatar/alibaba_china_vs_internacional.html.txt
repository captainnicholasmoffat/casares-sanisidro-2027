 Alibaba Cloud China Website vs. International Website 
 Alibaba Cloud China Website vs. International Website

 Compare the Alibaba Cloud China Website and the Alibaba Cloud International Website to choose the right site for your business.
 The Alibaba Cloud China Website (www.aliyun.com) and the Alibaba Cloud International Website (www.alibabacloud.com) operate separate account, billing, and legal compliance systems. The account data of the two sites is completely isolated and cannot be shared. Make sure that you register on the site that matches where your business is registered.
 Core differences 
 To help you decide, the following sections summarize the main differences in three areas: Account and verification , Billing and tax , and Products and services .

 Note 
 The Alibaba Cloud app only supports logging on with an Alibaba Cloud China Website account. You cannot use the app to log on to an Alibaba Cloud International Website account. If you use the International Website, go to the International Website (www.alibabacloud.com) or the International Website console to manage your account.
 To prevent confusion when users log on to either site, Alibaba Cloud checks the logon names of China Website accounts and the logon email addresses of International Website accounts to ensure that logon credentials are globally unique.

 FAQ 
 Can I change an Alibaba Cloud China Website account to an Alibaba Cloud International Website account? 
 No. The Alibaba Cloud China Website and the Alibaba Cloud International Website are two completely separate sites, and an account cannot be changed from one to the other. You can register a new account on the other site, and then migrate your business data and resources manually or by using tools.
 What are the differences when I purchase Chinese mainland resources on the Alibaba Cloud China Website vs. the Alibaba Cloud International Website? 
 There are three main differences:
 Billing and payment: With an Alibaba Cloud International Website account, all purchases are settled in USD or your local currency, and tax is calculated separately based on the tax policies of the country or region of your account. With an Alibaba Cloud China Website account, purchases are settled in CNY, and the prices of products and services include tax.
 Account ownership and compliance: Even if your account belongs to an Alibaba Cloud International Website legal entity, your purchase of Chinese mainland resources is still subject to the laws and regulations of the Chinese mainland. For example, you must complete real-name verification and ICP filing.
 Product updates: For the same region, the Alibaba Cloud China Website releases products and features tailored to the Chinese mainland market first, whereas the Alibaba Cloud International Website focuses on core cloud products for global use. Some products or features may differ between the two sites.
