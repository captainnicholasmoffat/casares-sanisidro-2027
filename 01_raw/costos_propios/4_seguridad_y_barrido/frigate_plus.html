Frigate+ Dataset

Get more relevant and accurate detections with Frigate+ models

Publicly available models are trained on standard datasets such as COCO which include somewhat random images from all sorts of scenarios. These models are trained to recognize things like gummy bears as bears.

Frigate+ models are trained using images from actual security cameras in use by Frigate users located all over the world.

Fine tune a model with your own image submissions

Accuracy can be further improved by uploading and annotating your own images and reporting false positives to create a model that is fine tuned to your specific camera scenes.

You will be able to request a fine tuned model with the images you have uploaded and annotated up to 12 times with your annual subscription. Additional fine tunings can be purchased for $5 if needed.

More objects for your automations

With Frigate+ models, you can use your cameras to detect if your waste bins are already out for trash day or if your robot lawnmower is running.

It is also trained to recognize common wildlife such as foxes, raccoons, squirrels, and deer in case you want a sprinkler head to turn on in a specific zone.

Track deliveries

With package detection, you can track when packages arrive at your front door. Frigate+ also recognizes common logos for delivery services all over the world, so you can know when UPS, Fedex, Amazon, or DHL have arrived.

More efficient face and license plate recognition

Frigate now supports facial and license plate recognition in version 0.16 (currently in beta). Frigate+ models make facial and license plate recognition more efficient by detecting the location of faces and license plates at the same time as other objects. Without a Frigate+ model, secondary models need to identify faces and license plates, slowing down your processing pipeline.
