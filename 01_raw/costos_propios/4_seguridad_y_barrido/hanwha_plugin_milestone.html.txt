 Download Image Hanwha Vision Plug-in for Milestone
 Official Integration Plugin for Milestone XProtect®
 Key Features
 This official plugin seamlessly integrates Hanwha Vision AI cameras with Milestone XProtect®, allowing you to leverage powerful AI analytics.
 AI camera-based object detection
 Support for similarity search 
 Control and management of various camera functions 
 Management of specialized PTZ functions (wiper, heater, spinning dry, and autofocus) 
 Metadata sharing between thermal and visible channels 
 Event integration for IP audio and intercom devices
 ※ Product availability may vary by region, please contact your regional representative.
 Download Product Images 
 AI Camera Integration 
 Full Power of AI Cameras,
Seamlessly Integrated
 The official plug-in connecting Hanwha Vision AI camera analytics with Milestone XProtect®. Leverage AI metadata, events, and camera controls directly from the interface you already use.
 Similarity Search 
 Match Persons Across Cameras
 Find objects with matching attributes across your stored AI event data. Select a target, set the time range, and quickly locate the same individual across multiple cameras.
 Object Detection 
 Expanded Range of Searchable Object Attributes
 Object support now spans eight categories for more precise, attribute-based search.
 Vehicle Management 
 Features for Distributed Systems
 Registering vehicles into groups under Vehicle Management enables custom event triggers upon license plate detection.
 Vehicle Management 
 IVA Overlays on Live Video
 Live video now supports Bounding Box, IVA area, and Virtual Line overlays. Compatible with both WiseAI app and camera firmware IVA for intuitive real-time viewing.
 Instant Alerts upon Event Detection
 IP Audio & Intercom Integration 
 Integrates with Hanwha Vision 's IP audio to broadcast pre-configured announcements upon events. It also supports answering intercom calls and controlling doors.
 Sound Classification 
 Enables real-time monitoring of sound classification events (such as screams, gunshots, or glass breakage) from supported cameras, instantly displaying a 'Sound Event' pop-up window.
