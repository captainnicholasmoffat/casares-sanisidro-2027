 Enhanced Audio Performance
 The Audio Beacon features a powerful 90dB speaker and five digital MEMS microphones for reliable, full-duplex two-way communication. High-volume alerts ensure critical messages are heard clearly, even in noisy or high-traffic environments. This audio clarity supports faster responses and improved situational awareness in real time.
 Visual Alerts with LED Ring
 A built-in LED ring delivers clear, customizable visual signals that can indicate different types of events. These high-visibility alerts help notify personnel and deter intruders in a wide range of lighting conditions. The LED’s dynamic color and pattern options make it a versatile tool for both awareness and deterrence.
 Two-way Audio & Speaker
 This innovative new product integrates microphones, speaker, and an LED ring, making it an ideal choice for professional audio applications. It uses AI-powered sound classification technology to classify the sound type, sound direction, and volume of sound. It features a speaker and LED ring for notification, as well as full-duplex two-way communication.
 Two-way Audio
 The Audio Beacon is equipped with built-in microphones and speaker, facilitating seamless communications. This product supports full duplex two-way audio, with echo cancellation and noise reduction.
 Audio File Playback Using Built-in Speaker
 Provides an instant notification when a specific sound is detected. When an AI audio event is detected, Audio Beacon can play a previously saved audio file, and it can play different sound file for each AI audio event.
 AI-Based Sound Classification
 AI-powered sound analysis detects predefined audio events like screams, glass breaking, and extreme decibel levels. This intelligent classification provides an added layer of awareness, helping operators identify and verify incidents faster.
 Seamless VMS Integration
 The Audio Beacon seamlessly integrates with VMS platforms, including Milestone and Genetec using Hanwha’s plugin, as well as Wisenet WAVE.
