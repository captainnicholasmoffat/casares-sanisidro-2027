How to purchase Milestone Care
1. Keep the original Milestone Care coverage period
An end-customer can maintain an existing Milestone Care renewal date when purchasing additional cameras, by purchasing Milestone Care 
coverage for the additional device licenses on a monthly basis (the required amount is automatically calculated in Milestone Store). This is 
useful if the main Milestone Care renewal date is aligned with the end customer’s calendar or fiscal year.
2. Extend the original Milestone Care coverage period
The end-customer can purchase one or more years of Milestone Care coverage when extending an existing system. This Milestone Care 
service coverage will be used to calculate a new and prolonged Milestone Care coverage period for the total system (still always expiring on 
the last day of the calculated month). The new Milestone Care coverage period is calculated based on the value of the remaining main 
Milestone Care coverage period and the size and nature of the system expansion.
If an end-customer does not want to purchase Milestone Care coverage for the system expansion after possible mandatory initial Milestone 
Care coverage period, the main Milestone Care will be terminated from the purchase date of the system expansion. Any remaining value of 
the main Milestone Care coverage period is not refunded. For details on how to ensure continuous Milestone Care coverage in conjunction 
with system expansions, please contact Milestone Sales Support.
A refund from trading-in software products cannot be converted to a cash payout, voucher etc. A negative value will instead be set to 0.00 
or can be used for purchase of other Milestone products within the same order.
Milestone Care Coverage in connection with upgrades
The following section is only applicable to XProtect VMS software products.
Milestone Care is linked to an individual system's base license. Milestone Care for system expansions must be aligned with or extend the 
Milestone Care of the existing installation. Please note that Milestone Care can never be shortened and will always expire on the last day of 
the calculated month. When expanding an existing XProtect system with additional cameras, the end-customer can maintain full Milestone 
Care coverage in two ways:
Milestone Care coverage is linked to a specific Milestone product. This means that end-customers upgrading to a more advanced product, 
for example when upgrading from XProtect Professional to XProtect Expert must also trade-in their Milestone Care in order to maintain 
coverage. To enable easy upgrades between different XProtect products, Milestone credits the price of the remaining unused Milestone 
Care coverage period to the price of the new Milestone Care. It is required to buy at least the same amount of Milestone Care as on the 
product being traded-in. However, if Milestone Care traded-in is less than one year, a minimum of one year is required on the new product.
Milestone Care Premium can be purchased at any time. However, Care Plus must be purchased as a prerequisite and the Care Premium 
expiration date cannot exceed that of the Care Plus. End customers can purchase Care Premium on any single license, just as they can Care 
Plus. 
How to purchase both Milestone Care Plus and Milestone Care Premium coverage in connection with system expansions of 
an XProtect VMS product 
To secure Care Plus coverage the service shall be purchased with the XProtect VMS product, or maximum 30 days after the original product 
purchase. XProtect VMS customers who want to join the Care Plus service at a later stage, or re-join after a period without coverage can do 
so using the three years opt-in option. There is also a grace period of 30 days from the expiration of an existing Milestone Care Plus 
coverage, in which the end-customer can purchase Milestone Care Plus under normal terms.
The minimum coverage period for both Milestone Care Plus and Care Premium is one year. Milestone Care can however also be purchased 
in increments of months, which is beneficial for installations that want to have their Care agreement(s) expire on a certain month (e.g. in 
alignment with a budgeting period). Milestone Care will always fall on the last day of the calculated month.
(Please note that for Milestone NVR products, Milestone Care coverage period is maximum 5 years from the date of purchase).

Trade-in
Please note that the terms outlined above reflect our current trade-in policy, which may be subject to change.
The present Milestone trade-in policy grants the customer a refund on the old product to be updated/upgraded, where the amount 
credited is dependent on the Milestone Care service level 
• Customers with active Milestone Care Plus are entitled to 100 percent refund of the current recommended retail price when trading-in 
licenses for their existing XProtect products.
• Customers without Milestone Care Plus coverage are entitled to 30 percent refund of the current recommended retail price of their 
existing product.
A refund from trading in software products cannot be converted to a cash payout, voucher etc., but will instead have to be used for 
purchase of other Milestone products.
The trade-in options vary slightly between the different Milestone products. For details, please refer to the trade-in options for the specific 
Milestone product in this price list.
Customers with Milestone Care Plus can instantly update their software to the latest version via the Customer Dashboard (also known as 
CUDA). Customers who want to upgrade to a more advanced product, or customers without Milestone Care Plus coverage who want to 
update their present Milestone product need to do that as an order transaction via Milestone Store.
Milestone currently offers a unique trade-in policy for our software products, which allows customers to protect investments made in 
Milestone software. The trade-in policy offers:
• Software update to a newer version of the same product
• Software upgrade to a more advanced product, for example upgrade from XProtect Expert to XProtect Corporate
