Notice of Emergency Rule
In order to more effectively deploy police resources during the FIFA World Cup and planned celebrations for the United States’ 250th anniversary, NYC Parks has issued an emergency rule to expand its discretion when reviewing permit applications for new events between June 11, 2026 and July 19, 2026.
Read the Full Details
Notice of Variance 
NYC Parks issued a rule variance allowing not-for-profit organizations who have allowed their IRS exempt status to lapse or who have applied for exempt status but wh have not received approval to not be subject to permit fees until October 31, 2025. 
Read the Full Details
Notice of Adoption
NYC Parks has amended its rules to remove the preference currently given to permit applications for events that were not held in the prior calendar year for large-size multi-day events at Flushing Meadows Corona Park.
Read the Full Details
Notice of Adoption 
NYC Parks has amended its rules to expand the categories of illegal behavior for which the Department's Enforcement Officers can issue a notice of violation (sometimes called a summons) in order to protect the natural environments under the jurisdiction of the department. 
Read the Full Details
Notice of Adoption
NYC Parks has amended its rules to prohibit vendors from storing property used for vending on sidewalks adjacent to parks and other property within the jurisdiction of the department. 
Read the Full Details
Notice of Variance Extension
NYC Parks is extending its rule variance allowing electric bikes and electric scooters to use park drives and greenways until December 31, 2025. 
Read the Full Details
Expressive Matter Rules
Find out answers to frequently asked questions about rules for selling paintings, prints, photography, reading materials, and sculpture, or for providing entertainment in exchange for a fee or donation in City parks.
Read the Full Details
Smoke-Free Parks and Beaches
Smoking is prohibited at all parks, beaches, and pedestrian plazas. Visit the Smoke-Free Parks page to find out more about this new legislation.
