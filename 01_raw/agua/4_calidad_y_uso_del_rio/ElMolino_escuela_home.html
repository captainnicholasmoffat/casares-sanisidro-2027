WHO WE ARE

 

We are the team of riders who will teach you how to sail safely to fully enjoy the sport you want to practice!

We founded El Molino in San Isidro, as a space to sail, feel the wind, have a good time and improve every day. We are passionate about what we do. Fun, emotion, learning and good vibes, are the pillars that determine us as a team.

It does not matter if you are a beginner or an expert. Our students are part of El Molino. For more than 10 years we have provided the best classes services with professional certified and trainer instructors.

We are explorers and thrillseekers, and we want you to join us.
