I.
PROGRAM STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING
1.
This Restructuring Paper seeks to provide a one-time policy waiver to the inclusion of the contract for the 
construction of the third module of the Norte Wastewater Treatment Plant under the Buenos Aires Water Supply and 
Sanitation with a Focus on Vulnerable Areas Program’s (the Program) activities and expenditure framework. The policy 
waiver has been approved by the Managing Director, Operations on December 13, 2023. 
2.
The Program’s progress towards the achievement of objectives as well as implementation progress are rated 
satisfactory, with US$89.37 million disbursed (29.8 percent) and a large share of the works contracts to achieve the 
associated results already signed. All program activities are making progress towards the achievement of Disbursement 
Linked Indicators (DLIs), implementation of household connections in areas with vulnerable households has gained 
momentum and are expected to continue to move at an accelerated pace.  
3.
All actions of the Program Action Plan have been complied with. All the ongoing works have their respective 
environmental permits approved. AySA has further improved the Program’s grievance redress mechanism with a 
dedicated channel for grievances under the program for connection of vulnerable households to the sewerage network 
and has recently implemented a protocol for dealing with situations of gender violence and gender discrimination 
under the program for connection of vulnerable households to the sewerage network. According to the reporting 
received from AySA, there have been no credible and material allegations on Fraud and Corruption under the Program. 
4.
This restructuring is proposed to allow for an exception to include in the Program’s activities and expenditure 
framework a high-value contract for the construction of the third module of the Norte Wastewater Treatment Plant 
under the Program. This restructuring is pursuant to paragraph 10 of Section III of the Program for Results Policy, which 
allows the inclusion of such contract if deemed important to the integrity of the Program and its monetary value in 
relation to the overall Program is modest.1 
5.
The Legal Agreement for the Program stipulates the exclusion of contracts that exceed the cost of US$75 million 
or more.2 The construction of the third module of the Norte Wastewater Treatment Plant was initially estimated to cost 
US$72.6 million. The contract is in the process of being awarded for the cost of approximately US$97.5 million (using 
the official exchange rate of ARS365.5 to US$1 of August 31, 20233), which exceeds the threshold. 
6.
The construction of the third module of the Norte Wastewater Treatment Plant is a key component of the 
project, as it will expand its treatment capacity from 1.8 m3/s to 2.8 m3/s and allow AySA to expand sanitation coverage 
for an additional 248,000 people in the Metropolitan Area of Buenos Aires. These works are directly linked to Results 
Area 1.2, in which the Program will expand safely managed sewerage services in selected municipalities of peri-urban 
Buenos Aires.
1 According to Section III, Paragraph 6 of the Bank Directive for Program for Results Financing, “unless determined otherwise by 
Management, an individual contract is deemed to be modest in relation to the overall PforR Program if its estimated monetary value 
is equal to or less than 25 percent of the estimated total PforR Program expenditures.”
2 The Loan Agreement (Section-II.B of Schedule-2) for the Buenos Aires Water Supply and Sanitation with a Focus on Vulnerable 
Areas Program for Results listed exclusion thresholds as follows: (i) works, estimated to cost US$75 million equivalent or more per 
contract; (ii) goods, information technology and non-consulting services estimated to cost US$50 million equivalent or more per 
contract; (iii) consulting services, estimated to cost US$20 million equivalent or more per contract.
3 Date of contract pre-award.

7.
Following Program design, the construction of the third module of the Norte Wastewater Treatment Plant is 
directly linked to the DLI 2.2: Number of households gaining access to safely managed sanitation services living in non-
fragile/formal neighborhoods of peri-urban Buenos Aires.4 About 57 percent of the value of this DLI (US$28 million) is 
contingent upon the successful implementation of this contract. This is critical for the achievement of the Program 
development objective to increase access to safely managed sanitation services. 
8.
The contract for the construction of the third module of the Norte Wastewater Treatment Plant represents 16.9 
percent of the April 2023 Program costs adjusted to reflect the higher value of this contract (i.e., US$577.9 million), 
which is below the threshold established in the Bank Directive for Program for Results Financing for contracts of 
modest value (less than 25 percent of the estimated total Program for Results expenditures). 
9.
The cost increases of this contract are a result of: (a) under-estimation of costs by 16 percent (primarily related 
to the cost of electromechanical equipment), (b) high inflation: according to the Argentinian National Institute of 
Statistics and Census (INDEC), by the date of bid submission the inflation index for the construction industry in 
Argentina increased by 494 percent since the costs were calculated in August 2020 for project appraisal, and (c) market 
distortions caused by the tight government regulation of the dollar exchange rate. The proposed contract amount is 
deemed reasonable compared with market prices. AySA has indicated it has the capacity to finance the increased 
Program Costs resulting from this higher value from its own resources. 
10.
The procurement process was carried out according to AySA’s procurement framework, procedures, and 
practices, including clearances and required approvals, which have been evaluated and found acceptable during 
Program preparation. It also follows the agreements established in the Program Action Plan.
II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES
11.
This restructuring is to allow for a one-time contractual waiver to the limitation set out in Section II.B(1) of 
Schedule 2 to the Loan Agreement. Therefore, activities which involve the procurement of works for the construction of 
the Third Module of the Norte Wastewater Treatment Plan in Buenos Aires (i.e., contrato de obras de ampliación de la 
Planta de Tratamiento Norte – 3º Modulo (NC70031)) are to be considered part of the Program.
 
4 DLI 2.2: Households in non-fragile/formal neighborhoods that will gain access to safely managed sanitation through the expansion 
of primary wastewater infrastructure (wastewater treatment and sewerage) in 7 municipalities of peri-urban Buenos Aires. The total 
amount allocated to this DLI is US$49.16 million.
