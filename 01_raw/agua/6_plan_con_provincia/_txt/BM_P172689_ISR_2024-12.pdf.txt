1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE
1.1 Development Objective
Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 24-Feb-2021)
The Program Development Objectives are to increase access to safely managed water and sanitation services with a focus 
on vulnerable areas of Buenos Aires, and improve AySA´s efficiency and capacity to respond to water and sanitation 
emergency needs
